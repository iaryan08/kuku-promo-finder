PROJECT SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Problem: Automate the discovery and ranking of 20s-60s promo clips across 
  50 microdrama episodes of "I Married a Broken Soldier. He was a Duke".
- Hybrid Architecture: "Code owns the clock, the LLM owns the drama."
  1. Deterministic Regex Parser: Extracts timecodes, reduces chars by 28%.
  2. Event-level Window Slider: Builds 565 candidate windows (20.0s to 60.0s).
  3. Structured Evaluator: Gemini 3.8 Flash JSON scoring (Hook, Cliffhanger, 
     Standalone) with automated fallback cascade and on-device Drama Heuristic 
     Engine for 100% offline confidential execution (--confidential).
  4. Temporal Deduplication: Ranks top 16 non-overlapping viral cuts.
